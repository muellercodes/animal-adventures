# Animal Adventures

A game by Rosemary. Pick an animal, eat food, earn leaves, and explore six worlds in 3D. It runs in the browser on a tablet, phone or computer.

**Play it:** https://muellercodes.github.io/animal-adventures/

## How it plays

- **Start animals:** duck, swan, chicken, tree frog, lizard (black with yellow spots), garter snake and bunny. All of them are free, and you can switch any time.
- **Growing up:** every new animal starts as a baby. Birds, frogs, lizards and turtles hatch from an egg (tap to crack it open), and the others are born. Babies grow by eating, and a bird has to be more than half grown before it can fly. You can give each animal a nickname.
- **Real animal moves:** bunnies, frogs and squirrels hop, snakes slither, ducks waddle and paddle, and the duck, swan, owl and parrot can fly. Animals that swim can go in the water, where the fish are. Frogs, lizards, squirrels and raccoons can climb trees.
- **Taking care of your animal:** your tummy goes down over time. If it stays empty too long your animal catches a cold (sneezing, no running or flying) until it eats, sleeps or sees the vet.
- **Food:** apples fall under apple trees, acorns under oaks, pine cones under pines, berries by bushes, carrots grow in the garden and fish swim in the ponds. Food your animal can eat glows. Walk up to it and tap Eat. Every animal has a favorite food that's worth more. Food your animal can't eat does nothing to you.
- **Leaves:** leaves are the money in the game. You earn them by eating (1 leaf, or 2 for a favorite) and by finding a new world for the first time (10 leaves).
- **Shop:** spend leaves on 14 more animals, from a squirrel (150 leaves) up to a horse and a moose. The others are a hedgehog, weasel, turtle, pig, goat, fox, otter, raccoon, owl, deer and parrot. There's also a pretend leaf store where you can "buy" leaf packs. A grown-up has to answer a times-table question first, and no real money is involved.
- **Worlds:** Land (a farm with a barn, fenced pens, a garden, orchard, pond and lake, plus a village on Maple Street), Sky Lands (floating islands, only for grown-up animals that fly), Forest, Swamp, Jungle and Meadow.
- **Endless worlds:** every world keeps going in every direction. The hand-made part is in the middle, and past it the game makes new woods, fields, lakes, ponds, giant trees, orchards and farms (with a barn, a farmhouse and penned animals) as you go. In the Map, tapping the world you're in takes you back to its start.
- **Wild animals:** each world has wild animals that wander, graze, rest at night and run (or fly) away if something big comes at them too fast. Out in the wilds new ones turn up around you. Play as the same kind of animal and some of them will tag along with you.
- **Farm animals and fences:** horses live in a paddock, pigs in a muddy pen, goats in a pen with a climbing platform and chickens in a coop run. Fences stop animals (fliers go over), and gates swing open when you walk up to them.
- **Day and night:** a day lasts 12 minutes and a night lasts 10. The sun moves across the sky, sunsets glow, and at night the stars and fireflies come out. Owls can see better in the dark.
- **People:** friendly people walk around the Land and the Meadow. Animals can't talk to people, so nobody asks you anything. Someone who likes your animal kneels down and holds out a hand. Walk up to them and they pet you, then they head home: follow them and they adopt you. Walking away means no. Your person follows you around, pets you and brings you snacks.
- **Homes:** when someone adopts you, you get a home in the village. Like real pets, the lizard, frog, snake and turtle live in a glass tank, the parrot in a big bird cage, and the bunny and hedgehog in an indoor pen. Other small animals are indoor pets with a bed. Farm animals (duck, swan, chicken, pig, goat, deer, horse, moose) live in a pen in the back yard. Every home has a food bowl. The Home button takes you there, and you can sleep at home to skip the night.
- **Town life:** the people of Maple Street have jobs. In the morning they leave home: some walk out of town to work and come back in the evening, the teacher and the kids go to school (go inside and you can sit in on class), and at recess and after school the kids play on the swings, slide, sandbox and seesaw and run over to pet animals they see. The farmer picks carrots in his garden, feeds his animals and checks his traps, and the fisher checks the fish traps in the pond and the lake. At night everyone goes home.
- **Traps:** weasels and other pests sneak into the farmer's garden to eat his carrots, so he keeps wire box traps baited with a carrot around it. When a pest walks in, the door snaps shut and the farmer takes it to the animal pound. Small animals can get caught too, so watch where you walk. Fish swim into the fish traps (look for the orange floats).
- **Animal control:** if a small animal walks around town without its person, Officer Kim may try to catch it and take it to the pound. Run away, or stay with your person or in your own yard. At the pound, a pet with a home has a collar: the pound calls the number on it and your person comes to take you home. Without a collar you wait in a kennel until someone adopts you (or tap Let me out).
- **Adoption fair:** by the meadow path there's an adoption fair with tents, balloons and pens. A scout looks in the woods for animals that don't have a home, like a wild otter or snake, and brings them to the fair, and people come by to adopt them. If your animal doesn't have a home, the scout may kneel down for you too, or you can walk into an empty pen and wait for someone to come meet you.
- **Houses, barns and the animal pound:** doors swing open and you walk through. You can go into the houses during the day and into barns any time (horse stalls, hay, a loft ladder). Big animals don't fit through house doors, but they fit in barns. At the end of Maple Street is the animal pound, with animals in cages, kennels and a vet who makes sick animals better. Go into a kennel and wait, and a visitor may come and kneel at the door; walk out to them to go home with them.
- **Holidays:** in the fall the leaves change color and drift down. From October 1 to November 2 there are pumpkins and jack-o'-lanterns, and at night you can go trick or treating at the houses for candy.
- **Playing together:** when you're signed in, the game is always online. Everyone else who is signed in and in the same world shows up as their animal, walking, swimming and flying around with you, and you hear their Call. Their label only says what animal they are (never a name), nothing anyone types is shared, and there is no chat. The paw pill at the top shows how many other players are in your world.
- **Sound:** animal calls, footsteps, wind, birds, crickets, frogs and a little music, all made by the game itself. Both can be turned off in Settings.

## Controls

On a phone or tablet: drag on the left side to walk (push farther to run), drag on the right side to look around, pinch to zoom. Tap Jump, and tap it again in the air to fly. While flying, hold Flap to go up and Land to come down. A purple button shows up when there's something to do right where you are (Go in, Go out, Go home, Sleep, Climb, Let go, Let me out, Trick or treat, See the vet). Call makes your animal's sound.

On a computer: WASD or the arrow keys to walk, Shift to walk slowly, Space to jump (press again in the air to fly, hold to flap up), E to eat, F to do the purple-button thing, V to call, C to land, drag with the mouse to look around and scroll to zoom.

## Saves

The game saves on the device by itself, and a small "Saved" sign shows when it does. If you make an account (a name and a password, no email), your game also saves online so you can keep playing on another device. The newest save wins.

The backend is a tiny [Cloudflare Worker](worker/src/index.js) with a [D1](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/) (SQLite) database and one [Durable Object](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/) per world that relays where everyone's animal is over WebSockets, the same setup as [Emberblood](https://github.com/muellercodes/emberblood). It fits in Cloudflare's free tier and doesn't get paused or deleted when nobody plays for a while. Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256, sessions are random tokens stored only as hashes, and nothing personal is collected.

## Project layout

```
game/shell.html         page markup and styles
game/src/00_core.js     game data (animals, food, prices) and helpers
game/src/35_envkit.js   3D scenery: trees, plants, barn, grass, water, sky, clouds, food
game/src/35_envkit.js   (also houses and rooms, the pound, the school, a playground, traps, the adoption fair, pens, pumpkins, nests and eggs)
game/src/40_animals.js  the 21 animals as 3D models (grown-up and baby) with walk, hop, swim, fly, climb and eat animation
game/src/45_people.js   the people
game/src/50_world.js    the six endless worlds: terrain tiles, water, where things grow, landmarks, the village, pens and gates
game/src/60_actors.js   movement, wild animal and people behavior, homes, food
game/src/62_town.js     town life: jobs, school, traps, animal control, the pound calling your person, the adoption fair
game/src/65_audio.js    all the sounds and music (Web Audio, made in code)
game/src/68_net.js      playing together: sends your animal to the server and draws everyone else
game/src/70_game.js     rendering, camera, controls, day and night, menus, saves
game/vendor/            three.js r149
game/assets/            pre-rendered menu pictures (made by tools/assets.py)
game/models/            sculpted animal models (made by tools/blender, embedded by build.mjs)
tools/blender/          the Blender pipeline that sculpts, bakes and exports those models
game/config.json        address of the cloud save API
build.mjs               builds docs/index.html
docs/                   the published site (GitHub Pages serves this folder)
worker/                 Cloudflare Worker API + D1 schema
```

Everything 3D is built from code when the game loads, using [three.js](https://threejs.org). The one exception is the pig, whose body is sculpted by script in Blender (see [tools/blender](tools/blender/README.md)) and embedded in the page as a small mesh with color and normal maps.

## Building

```bash
node build.mjs                                   # uses game/config.json
API_BASE=http://127.0.0.1:8787 node build.mjs    # point at a local worker
API_BASE= node build.mjs                         # no cloud saves
```

Then open `docs/index.html`, or serve the folder (`npx serve docs`).

To redraw the animal icons and world pictures in the menus after changing a model or a world:

```bash
NO_ASSETS=1 API_BASE= node build.mjs && python3 tools/assets.py && node build.mjs
```

## Running the backend

```bash
cd worker
npm install
npx wrangler login
npx wrangler d1 create animal-adventures        # copy the database_id into wrangler.toml
npx wrangler d1 execute animal-adventures --remote --file=schema.sql
npx wrangler deploy
```

The live game uses `https://animal-adventures-api.muellercodes.workers.dev`. Allowed browser origins are set by `ALLOWED_ORIGINS` in `wrangler.toml` (localhost is always allowed for testing).
